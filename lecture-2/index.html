Quick reminder

What is a factorial?

Multiply all the numbers from 1 up to the number.

4! = 1 × 2 × 3 × 4 = 24

5! = 1 × 2 × 3 × 4 × 5 = 120

In Lecture 1 we found one factorial with a loop. Today we find several and add them up.

  1. 1

    Add factorials together

    Work out sums like 8! + 6! + 4!, one part at a time.

  2. 2

    Use two loops

    One loop finds a factorial. A second loop moves on to the next one.

  3. 3

    Start fresh each time

    Before the next factorial, set fact back to 1.

The big idea

A loop inside a loop

To work out y = 8! + 6! + 4!, we do the same thing for each number:

Outer loop: i = 8, then 6, then 4

  1. i = 81 × 2 × 3 × 4 × 5 × 6 × 7 × 8= 40,320add to y
  2. i = 61 × 2 × 3 × 4 × 5 × 6= 720add to y
  3. i = 41 × 2 × 3 × 4= 24add to y

y = 40,320 + 720 + 24 = 41,064

Inner loop multiplies 1 × 2 × 3 … to find one factorial.

Outer loop moves to the next number and starts again with fact = 1.

Wrap-up

Four things to remember

  1. 1

    The inner loop builds a factorial

    fact = fact * c, c = c + 1, repeat while c <= i.

  2. 2

    The outer loop moves to the next term

    Change the counter, then reset fact = 1 and c = 1 before the next factorial.

  3. 3

    The step follows the series

    i − 2 for 8, 6, 4 · i + 3 for 2, 5, 8 · d + 2 for 3, 5, 7.

  4. 4

    Reuse what you already have

    (i + 2)! comes from i! in Example 3. x! is worked out only once in Example 4.
